Subject: You're in! Harmonia's Android beta is ready 🎵

Android beta

You're in! 🎧

Thanks for signing up to test Harmonia. You hear a short piece of music, then rebuild it by choosing which instruments play and which note each one plays. Your spot in the Android beta is ready.

Join the beta →

Open this on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you signed up with.

Getting started

1 Tap Join the beta, then Become a tester.
2 Install Harmonia from the Play Store link on that page. The button can take a few minutes to show up.
3 Play! Keep it installed and open it now and then.
Please stay in the beta for at least 14 days. Google only lets Harmonia launch publicly after testers stay opted in for 14 days in a row, so you're helping it get out the door.
What to try and how to report bugs are in the tester guide.
Bugs, ideas, or just what you thought: feedback@playharmonia.app

Good to know

🛒 Purchases are free. Anything you buy in the beta is a test purchase, so you won't be charged.

📺 Please don't tap the ads on purpose. You'll see real ads between some levels, and tapping them on purpose can get the ad account flagged.

Thanks so much for helping out. Have fun, and see how good your ears really are!
