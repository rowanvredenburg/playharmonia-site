Thanks for testing Harmonia

Harmonia is an ear-training puzzle: you hear a short piece of music, then rebuild it by choosing which instruments play and which note each one plays.

Joining

  1. Open the invite link from your email on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you gave us, and tap Become a tester.
  2. Install Harmonia from the Play Store link on that page. The install button can take a few minutes to appear after you join.
  3. Keep the game installed and open it now and then.

Please stay in the beta for at least 14 days. Google only lets the game launch publicly after testers have stayed opted in for 14 days in a row.

What to try

You don't need to do everything. Whatever you get to helps.

Playing

  • Play the first 10 levels or so. Do the instruments, sliders and buttons feel right? Is anything cut off by the camera hole or the screen edges?
  • Drag a note slider quickly back and forth. Any clicks or pops?
  • Finish a level. Do the rewards fly into the counters at the top? Does a tap skip it? Is anything hard to read?
  • Try for a Perfect!: first guess, no hints, no replays.

Daily Challenge

  • Play today's daily, then tap Review, listen to each instrument, and tap Results to go back.
  • Tap Share on the daily result.

Android

  • Use your phone's back gesture everywhere. It should close pop-ups and go back one screen, and only close the game from the main menu.
  • Turn on reminders (in Settings, or the prompt after your first daily win). Let us know if one arrives around noon or 8 pm on a day you haven't played the daily.
  • Leave the game mid-level and come back later. Are you where you left off, with the sound still working?
  • In the Shop, buy Remove Ads. Beta purchases are free test purchases ("Test card, always approves").
  • Uninstall and reinstall. Are ads still off? Try Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Shop.

You'll see real ads between some levels. Please don't tap them on purpose, since that can get the ad account flagged. Do tell us if an ad breaks the sound or the game.

Sending feedback

Found a bug, or have thoughts? Email us. Please include:

  • what you did and what happened (a screenshot or screen recording is great),
  • your phone model and Android version (Settings › About phone).

Tell us what you liked, what confused you, and whether you'd keep playing, too. That's just as useful.
